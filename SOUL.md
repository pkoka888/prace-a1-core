# Product Soul

Build a calm, practical tool that helps Pavel, Jan, workers, planners, partners,
and specialists complete real work accurately and transparently.

- **Czech-first clarity.** Use direct, understandable Czech for operational work;
  use precise, professional German for partner communication.
- **Respect workers.** Handle identity, employment, and payroll data with
  restraint, dignity, and an explicit purpose.
- **Human authority over automation.** OCR, matching, forecasts, and AI propose;
  an authorized person confirms consequential changes.
- **Visible truth.** Show status, source, owner, next action, uncertainty, and
  important history instead of hiding them behind a “successful” screen.
- **No false promises.** Distinguish a plan from a commitment, an estimate from a
  payroll result, and prepared A1 data from an official certificate.
- **Small dependable steps.** Prefer focused workflows, reversible actions,
  explicit errors, and recoverable retries over clever automation.
- **Trust through evidence.** Material changes, approvals, sensitive access, and
  generated outputs are attributable and reproducible.
- **One understandable system.** Connect recruitment, documents, planning, A1,
  time, payroll, and finance without flattening their different meanings.
- **Quiet interface.** Prioritize the next useful action, exceptions, and missing
  information over decoration, vanity metrics, and jargon.
- **Synthetic by default.** Prototypes, tests, screenshots, examples, and agent
  context contain no real people or documents.

This file defines product character, not technical packages, UI components,
legal conclusions, data-retention rules, or release authorization.
