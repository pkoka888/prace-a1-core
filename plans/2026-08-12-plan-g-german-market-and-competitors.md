# Plan G — German Market and Competitor Research

**Status:** active
**Milestone:** M1 input and M6 decision support
**Owner:** product research

## Objective

Establish evidence for target German employers, occupations, regions, competitors, and product differentiation without delaying the Jan pilot.

## Method

- Use Bundesagentur für Arbeit and other primary statistics to identify shortages by occupation, skill level, region, and trend.
- Separate direct Czech/Slovak-to-Germany recruiters from general job boards, staffing/temporary-work firms, ATS products, official mobility portals, and adjacent services.
- For each serious competitor record identity, URL, countries, target customer, occupations, service model, employer/candidate workflow, document/A1 assistance, trust claims, public pricing, supported integrations, strengths, weaknesses, source type/date, and confidence.
- Verify official posting/feed routes separately from consumption. Treat HR-BA-XML, EURES, and portal APIs as M6 topics and never infer permission from an accessible endpoint.
- Interview the operator and a small set of German employers to validate problems, buying process, existing alternatives, objections, and decision criteria.

## Deliverables

- Evidence-backed market map and direct-competitor matrix.
- Prioritized occupation/region hypotheses.
- German employer personas and buying workflow.
- Differentiation hypotheses tied to observed evidence.
- Source register distinguishing primary, official, vendor, secondary, inaccessible, and inferred claims.
- Follow-up questions and M6 integration register.

## Exit gate

Every published claim has an appropriate source; Kimeta and other general portals are correctly classified; unsupported rankings and scale claims are absent; interviews confirm or reject the leading opportunity hypotheses.

## Exclusions

No scraping, feed implementation, price commitment, market-size invention, or public-site build.
