# Plan I — Marketing and Business Validation

> **Superseded — do not execute as a Release 1A gate.** Retained as reviewed input
> for later M1B/M6 business and public-growth work in [`../roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md).

**Status:** superseded
**Milestone:** M1 input and M6 prerequisite
**Owner:** product owner

## Objective

Replace unsupported channel, pricing, and positioning statements with testable commercial evidence.

## Work

- State separate candidate and German-employer value propositions as hypotheses.
- Interview representative candidates, Jan/operator, and German employers about acquisition routes, trust, objections, buying authority, existing costs, willingness to pay, and service expectations.
- Define small experiments for SEO/search, referrals, community partnerships, employer outreach, industry networks, paid search, and compliant social channels.
- For every experiment predefine audience, message, landing action, budget ceiling, success metric, privacy/terms constraint, stop condition, and attribution method.
- Compare placement fee, subscription, service fee, and hybrid models using evidence; record VAT/accounting/legal questions separately.

## Deliverables

- Positioning and revenue hypotheses with evidence/confidence.
- Candidate and employer acquisition funnels.
- Prioritized experiment backlog and measurement dictionary.
- Assumption model for cost, conversion, placement value, retention, and payback.
- Decision memo selecting only validated experiments for M6.

## Exit gate

No channel is labeled effective and no price/revenue model is selected without measured or interview evidence. The first M6 experiments have owners, budgets, metrics, terms checks, and stop conditions.

## Exclusions

No ad spend, automated posting, binding pricing, unsupported market sizing, or collection of candidate PII during research.
