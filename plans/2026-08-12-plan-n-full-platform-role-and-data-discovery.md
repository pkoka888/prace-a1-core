# Plan N — Full-Platform Role, Workflow, and Data Discovery

**Status:** active
**Prerequisite:** The operational Plan L/M baseline is sufficient for synthetic
Release 1A catalog work. Plan M's broader exit and Jan's operational validation
remain required before Plan N acceptance, not before drafting and proof work.
**Milestone:** M1A–M1B
**Owner:** product owner + domain research

## Objective

Design the complete platform now—recruitment, workforce, partner operations,
planning, attendance, A1, payroll, and operational finance—while preserving Jan's
workflow as the first deployable increment.

## Release 1A slice — critical path

- Freeze the Jan/operator role, permission, field, report, data-necessity,
  authoritative-source, data-quality-state, and acceptance catalog required by
  M1A; broader platform discovery continues afterward.
- The synthetic M2 slice is frozen in
  `docs/architecture/release-1a-catalog.json` using the delegated recommended
  defaults. The document's exception-only list preserves unresolved operational
  facts for Jan; broader Plan N remains active.

## Broader platform slice — parallel, non-blocking

- Research mature systems and regulations before role interviews; compare useful
  patterns without copying an ERP wholesale.
- Prototype synthetic role dashboards and end-to-end journeys for director,
  Jan/planner, worker/candidate, German organization, operator, payroll specialist,
  accountant/finance, and administrator/privacy.
- During walkthroughs capture actions, decisions, questions, reports, filters,
  exports, permissions, exceptions, approvals, handoffs, and source authority.
- Maintain a field-necessity catalog: `must`, `should`, `later`, or `rejected`, with
  role/purpose/source/provenance/ownership/sensitivity/effective dates/validation/
  retention/migration mapping.
- Specify states and information needs for jobs, applications/reservations,
  workers/profiles/contracts, documents/OCR, workplaces/assignments/schedules,
  attendance/time, A1, payroll, finance, communication, and rights/incidents.

## Exit

Pavel/Jan first approve the Release 1A catalog needed for M1A, then approve or annotate the synthetic whole-platform views;
every retained field and report has a role, purpose, authority, and delivery
increment; open legal/payroll facts are explicit rather than guessed.
Release 1A catalog slice is approved first; broader M1B discovery continues afterward.
