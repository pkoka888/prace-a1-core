# Operator A1 workflow contract

Status: specified; interface implementation follows stabilization acceptance.
Parents: PLAN-WORKERS and PLAN-A1; existing A1-REPAIR-3 and J4 tasks remain the
execution anchors. This document does not introduce another parser or state model.

## Facts, confirmation and authority

The system performs classification, side matching, field validation, duplicate
checks, retries and missing-evidence routing automatically. Local and web adapters
consume the same semantic results. Uncertain identity evidence remains unassigned;
names, filenames and upload order cannot alone establish identity.

Jan supplies missing facts and resolves conflicting proposals using the visible
source evidence. Present one consolidated confirmation of the reviewed information;
record the authorized actor, accepted revision and provenance. Technical settings,
OCR backend, storage paths and legal article/profile selection are not operator
choices. A later changed fact invalidates dependent evidence rather than silently
reusing an earlier confirmation.

Collect actual employer, working arrangements, assignment period and countries.
Keep registered address separate from habitual residence; neither is automatically
the other. The official application instructions distinguish these concepts:
[ČSSZ application instructions](https://eportal.cssz.cz/documents/20122/35811/SZZVPP_pokyny.pdf/57daae1d-f7a8-7512-c5c1-7a3b5f9e10ca?t=1652879516928).

## Application preparation is distinct from certificate receipt

ČSSZ processes applications and issues A1 certificates:
[ČSSZ submission and issuance guidance](https://www.cssz.gov.cz/web/cz/podani-zadosti-o-urceni-prislusnosti-883-2004-987-2009).
Application readiness requires accepted identity/activity and assignment facts,
including a requested future period where applicable. It cannot require Jan to
invent an issued certificate number, certified dates or official signatory.

Authorization to represent/submit, actual submission, fact confirmation and receipt
of the issued certificate are separate recorded events. A prepared application or
client confirmation copy is never described as an issued A1. Certificate readiness
continues to require the canonical authoritative evidence and acceptance gates.
Do not weaken the current certificate renderer to prepare an application.

Versioned, source-backed rules may route an established fact pattern. Unresolved
legal applicability goes to the competent institution, with a concise missing-facts
summary; do not ask Jan to choose Article 12 versus Article 13 or guess a checkbox.

## Subsequent interface acceptance

Use existing worker/intake/A1 records and states where applicable; implement the
application adapter as a separately accepted deliverable under existing tasks.
Demonstrate mixed documents, front-only input, misleading filenames, conflicting
identifiers, invalid dates, enhancement disagreement, future assignments, separate
residence concepts and no invented issuance fields. Jan sees missing/conflicting
facts and one confirmation summary; authorization/submission remain independently
traceable. No new UI, real-data processing or official submission is authorized by
this specification alone.
